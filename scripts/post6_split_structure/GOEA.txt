Functional check of the 8 strongest split-6 genes (STRING network + InterPro enrichment)
=======================================================================================

WHERE THE 8 GENES COME FROM
- Data: Froussios et al. 2019, ExpA, coding-gene counts (featureCounts -t gene, work/STAR_both/fC/).
- Comparison: post 5's split 6 = replicates 1, 2, 3 (group 1) vs. replicates 4, 5, 6 (group 2);
  same genotype, same batch, no designed difference; replicate 7 not used.
- Method: DESeq2 (default median-of-ratios size factors, design ~group), Wald test;
  log2FC shrunken with apeglm (lfcShrink, coef group_g2_vs_g1).
- Selection rule (post 7): padj < 0.05 AND |shrunken log2FC| > 2 (more than 4-fold).
  8 of split 6's 689 padj < 0.05 genes pass. log2FC > 0 = higher in replicates 4, 5, 6.

READING
The 8 genes are more connected than expected by chance (6 edges, PPI enrichment p = 7.31e-14)
and point to
carbon/energy status (EXL1, EXL5), auxin (YUC5), stress-responsive ERF transcription factors and
photoperiod/light signalling (MIP1A/BBX30, the only gene lower in group 2). Consistent with, but not
proof of, a harvest-time / light / energy-status difference following the replicate numbering
(post 6). 8 genes is a very small list: treat as a lead; the full 689-gene list needs its own GO
enrichment against the tested-gene background.
